# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, established by the approved concept implementation.

## Users

Current and prospective dental patients in and around Vračar, especially people who postpone dental care because they fear pain, judgment, unclear costs or rushed treatment.

## Product Purpose

The landing page presents Dental Studio Vračar and has one conversion goal: make the visitor call the practice to schedule a first appointment.

## Positioning

Calm, unhurried and clearly explained dental care, with a free first examination for new patients and the treatment plan and price explained before work begins.

## Operating Context

Visitors are likely evaluating the practice on a phone while looking for reassurance, practical information, transparent prices, location and a fast way to call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every call to action links to `tel:+381114042525`.
- No booking form and no email field.
- The site language is Serbian, Latin script.
- Required sections, copy, prices and business details come from the supplied brief and must not be changed.
- The location section includes a Google Maps embed for Njegoševa 42, Beograd.
- Mobile includes a fixed bottom call action.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dental Studio Vračar.
- Clean, bright, medical and calm tone.
- Fixed palette: white `#FFFFFF`, light blue `#EEF5FC`, blue `#1F6FD1`, text `#0F2A44`, secondary text `#5B6B7C`.
- Photography is photorealistic, bright, white and light blue, daylight, with no text or logos.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five generated project images in `assets/`: hero, dentist portrait, reception, equipment and smile.
- Business facts, testimonials, service descriptions and prices supplied directly by the user.
- No additional services, reviews, statistics or claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the phone call the obvious next action.
- Reduce anxiety through clarity and humane imagery.
- Present costs and process without ambiguity.
- Keep every interaction simple, accessible and mobile-ready.
