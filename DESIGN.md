# Design System

## Direction

“Mirno poverenje”: bright clinical minimalism with an architectural split hero, generous white space, precise dark-blue typography and human daylight photography.

## Color

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Light blue field: `#EEF5FC`
- Primary blue: `#1F6FD1`
- Ink: `#0F2A44`
- Secondary text: `#5B6B7C`
- Hairline: `#D8E4F0`

## Typography

Manrope for display, body and controls, with deliberate weight contrast and balanced line breaks. Display tracking never exceeds `-0.04em`.

## Geometry

Open sections, horizontal rules and image frames. Cards are reserved for the doctor statement, map information and review rail where containment has a clear purpose. Radii are 12–16px.

## Motion

One calm reveal language: slight upward travel with a short blur that resolves on entry. Interactive motion is limited to button lift, link underline/shift and accordion disclosure.

## Media

Generated photography is used only in its assigned section. Hero has no color overlay. All images use stable aspect ratios and natural crops.
